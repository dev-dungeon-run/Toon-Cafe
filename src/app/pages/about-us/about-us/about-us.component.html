Welcome to Toon Café

where coffee meets cartoons, and every sticker has a story.

Origin Story

Brewed in Mexico — or more specifically, on my desk — this is a vacation project by a CS/software student with a deep love for cartoons. Originally imagined as a sticker café, it quickly transformed into Toon Café — a cozy, make-believe spot where people come to watch cartoons, sip coffee, study, or just vibe out with some animated nostalgia.

The Vibe

To bring joy, nostalgia, and a little caffeine-fueled chaos into your day. Toon Café is a chill space where you can admire art on the walls, snack on treats, sip coffee, get work done, or just hang out watching cartoons. We even have computers for studying or gaming, and other imaginative goodies sprinkled around. It’s part coffee shop, part cartoon daydream, all love.
